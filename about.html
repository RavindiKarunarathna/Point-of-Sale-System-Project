About Keells

Welcome to Keells

Keells is proudly Sri Lankan, owned and operated for the past 33 years, delivering the freshest quality products along with great value, serving thousands of Sri Lankans every day.

As a subsidiary of the John Keells Group, Keells operates 135 outlets. With the core purpose of improving the quality of life for the nation, the supermarkets are conveniently located across the country, providing our shoppers with a world-class retail experience.

Our team of over 5,000 people is at the heart of our success and we strive to provide our customers with an enjoyable shopping experience and our stakeholders with long-lasting partnerships. With over 9 collection centres in strategic locations and a 260,000-square-foot state-of-the-art distribution centre, Keells sources fresh produce from farmers and dry products from suppliers, ensuring this produce reaches our stores within 24 hours. Our 130 SLSI-certified bakeries provide oven-fresh bread and bakery products, and our 66 GMP-certified hot kitchens offer busy customers convenient, ready-to-eat meals.

Our ever-growing own-label product portfolio includes over 300 items. Additionally, Keells offers a diverse range of more than 200 directly imported products, exclusively sourced and sold at our supermarkets, providing discerning customers with an extensive selection of choices. As the first supermarket in Sri Lanka to launch an online shopping platform, Keells offers home delivery and click-and-collect services, ensuring consistent access to the products our customers need. Our Nexus loyalty program, with over 2.4 million members, provides great deals and savings, rewarding customers as they shop.

Keells was the first retailer in Sri Lanka to commit to reducing single-use polythene bags and in-store prepared food packaging by 50% by 2025. We are committed to minimizing our environmental impact and employing sustainable practices as our business grows. Over 107 of our stores use solar power, relying more on renewable energy sources as part of our sustainability initiatives.

At Keells, we are proud to be a part of the lives of Sri Lankans, delivering quality, freshness, and value every day.
